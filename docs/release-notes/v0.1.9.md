- **Style rules in document order before jsdom 28.1** (#121, #139): in the jsdom 20 to 28.0 profiles, `getComputedStyle()` applies the page's style rules in document order, as jsdom does: selector specificity and `!important` in a rule don't count, the style attribute wins over every rule, and the user agent sheet sits under every page rule (a `display` rule shows an element with `hidden`). From 28.1 the cascade uses specificity as before. Before 30.1.2 style sheets are ordered by when they were created, not by their position in the document, and `@media all` applies only from jsdom 29. Measured on 18 jsdom releases from 20.0.3 to 30.1.3; suites that still run on jsdom 26 or 27 can read different computed styles from competing rules than with 0.1.8.
- **EventTarget methods without a receiver** (#128, #132): `addEventListener`, `removeEventListener` and `dispatchEvent` called detached (`const rm = el.removeEventListener; rm('x', f)`) act on the window, as in every jsdom from 20 to 30, instead of throwing `Illegal invocation`. Each window (and iframe window) has its own three functions.
- **Empty declarations in inline styles, jsdom 20 to 26** (#129, #132): a leading `;` or an empty declaration in `style.cssText` (`el.style.cssText += ';overflow: hidden;padding-right: 0px;'`) swallows the declaration after it, as cssstyle does in those releases; from jsdom 27 it doesn't. Style sheets drop the same declarations in those profiles.
- **The `background` shorthand per jsdom version** (#130, #132): parsing and serialization follow the four cssstyle generations (before 4.4, 4.4 to 4.6, jsdom 27 to 29, jsdom 30), for example `background: 0` is rejected in jsdom 23, `0px` in 26 and `0px center` in 30; numbers are written in their canonical form where jsdom does that, and invalid units are rejected.
- **`warpdom compare` matches tests whose name changes between runs** (#131, #135): tests with a timestamp or other varying text in their name are paired by file, describe path and position and compared, instead of being reported as flaky on both sides. They are listed as "name varies between runs" in the terminal, the report and the GitHub Action's summary. Ambiguous cases are left unpaired.
- Each change was checked on 19 open-source suites plus Ant Design, Mantine, Excalidraw and wire-webapp (GitHub Actions, jsdom vs warpdom): no test changed status apart from the known, documented differences. The three Mattermost changes take its channels suite from 49 tests that differ from jsdom to 0 (measured locally on two shards).
